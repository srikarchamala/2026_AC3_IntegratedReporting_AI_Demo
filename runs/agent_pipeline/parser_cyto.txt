G-banded chromosome analysis of the unstimulated bone marrow aspirate (24- and 48-hour synchronized cultures, 400-425 bphs) showed a normal male karyotype, reported verbatim as "46,XY[20]," with "No clonal numerical or structural abnormality was identified at the stated band resolution." Interphase FISH with the AML panel (200 nuclei scored per probe) was negative for every probe tested: RUNX1-RUNX1T1 t(8;21)(q22;q22) (0.5%), CBFB inv(16)/t(16;16)(p13;q22) (1.0%), PML-RARA t(15;17)(q24;q21) (0.5%), KMT2A (MLL) 11q23.3 rearrangement (1.5%), MECOM (EVI1) 3q26.2 rearrangement (1.0%), -5/5q- (EGR1) deletion 5q31 (1.5%), -7/7q- (D7S486) monosomy 7/del(7q) (1.0%), and TP53 (17p13.1) deletion 17p (2.0%), with all signal counts within the laboratory's normal cutoff range. No classifying recurrent cytogenetic abnormality was detected, and the report states that "This places the case in the cytogenetically normal category," which is itself a relevant finding for WHO 5e classification. The report states these limitations: "A normal karyotype does not exclude a submicroscopic abnormality"; results apply only at the stated band resolution with 20 cells counted and 5 fully karyotyped; and molecular testing (NPM1, FLT3, CEBPA, and a broader myeloid panel) "is required for classification and risk stratification" and has been ordered separately (accession MMD-26-NGS-22150).
