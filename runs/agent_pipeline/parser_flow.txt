Flow cytometry of the bone marrow aspirate (eight-color panel, CD45 vs side scatter gating; viability 94% by 7-AAD) identifies an abnormal myeloid blast population of approximately 22% of total nucleated cells, alongside a markedly expanded, left-shifted monocytic compartment of maturing monocytes 19% plus promonocytes/immature monocytic cells 11% (together approximately 30% of TNC). The blasts express CD34 (subset, ~60%), CD117, HLA-DR (bright), CD13, CD33 (bright), CD38, CD64, dim CD4 and dim cytoplasmic MPO, are CD14 negative, and are negative for CD2, CD19 and cyCD3; the monocytic population shows bright uniform CD64, CD14 on the maturing fraction, CD11b, CD11c, CD36, HLA-DR and dim CD4. The report states that this immunophenotype establishes the monocytic lineage that was only favored on morphology, resolving that lineage hedge, with no lymphoid lineage markers (CD2, CD19, cyCD3) expressed. Aberrant antigen expression is present on the blasts, with CD56 (partial) and CD7 (subset), which the report notes may be useful for measurable residual disease tracking. As limitations, the report notes that the flow blast estimate (22%) differs modestly from the manual aspirate differential (flow gating and manual counts are expected to differ, and either value is stated to support the diagnosis), that the assay is a laboratory-developed test, and that correlation with cytogenetic and molecular studies is required for final classification.
