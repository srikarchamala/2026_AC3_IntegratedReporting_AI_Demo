## 3. Summary by Study

### A. Morphology and cytochemistry (S26-BM-01187)
- **Aspirate (500-cell differential):** 18% blasts and 4% promonocytes (counted separately); 6% mature monocytes; M:E ratio about 4.4:1.
- Blasts are medium to large with fine chromatin and 1–3 nucleoli. A subset has folded or indented nuclei and grayish cytoplasm, which suggests monocytic features. **No Auer rods** were seen.
- **Core biopsy:** 90–95% cellular, with sheets of immature mononuclear cells. Residual hematopoiesis is markedly decreased and megakaryocytes are markedly reduced. Reticulin is MF-1. There are no ring sideroblasts.
- **Cytochemistry:** MPO stains about 5–10% of blasts. NSE is positive in a subset of cells with partial fluoride inhibition, which supports a monocytic component.
- **Diagnosis:** hypercellular marrow with acute myeloid leukemia, probably with monocytic differentiation. The aspirate blast count is likely underestimated because of hemodilution and because promonocytes are hard to tell apart from blasts on smears.

### B. Flow cytometry (FC-26-003942)
- Viability was 94%.
- **Abnormal myeloid blasts: 22% of total nucleated cells.** Phenotype: CD34+ (about 60%), CD117+, HLA-DR bright, CD13+, CD33 bright, CD38+, dim cytoplasmic MPO. Aberrant markers: CD56 (partial) and CD7 (subset). CD64+ and dim CD4. Negative for CD14, CD2, CD19 and cyCD3.
- **Expanded, left-shifted monocytic compartment, about 30% in total** (19% maturing monocytes plus 11% promonocytes). Phenotype: bright uniform CD64, CD14 on the maturing fraction, CD11b, CD11c, CD36, HLA-DR, dim CD4, and partial CD56.
- Lymphocytes are polytypic with no aberrancy.
- **Interpretation:** AML with monocytic differentiation. Flow cytometry confirms the monocytic lineage that morphology could only suggest. The aberrant CD56/CD7 pattern is a possible marker for measurable residual disease (MRD).

### C. Cytogenetics and FISH (CG-26-00871)
- **Karyotype:** 46,XY[20]. This is a normal male karyotype; 20 metaphases were analyzed at 400–425 band resolution.
- **AML FISH panel (200 nuclei per probe):** negative for RUNX1-RUNX1T1, CBFB, PML-RARA, KMT2A, MECOM, −5/5q, −7/7q and TP53/17p deletion. All results were within normal cutoffs.

## 4. Integrated Interpretation

All four studies point to one diagnosis:

1. **Acute leukemia of myeloid lineage.** Morphology, MPO cytochemistry and the flow phenotype (CD34/CD117/CD13/CD33/MPO) all show this.
2. **Monocytic differentiation.** Morphology and NSE suggested it, and flow cytometry confirmed it with an expanded CD64-bright, CD14/CD11b/CD11c/CD36+ compartment.
3. **Blast counts differ by method.** The counts are 18% on the manual aspirate, 22% by flow, and 41% in peripheral blood, and the core biopsy shows extensive infiltration. The low aspirate count is explained by hemodilution and by promonocytes being counted separately. Promonocytes count as blast equivalents, so adding them (18% + 4%) puts the aspirate near the flow value. **The difference does not affect the diagnosis**, because mutated NPM1 defines this entity under WHO and ICC criteria without a 20% blast threshold, as the molecular report states.
4. **Genetics.** The karyotype and AML FISH panel are normal. The disease-defining lesion is a type A **NPM1** frameshift, with co-occurring **FLT3-ITD** and **DNMT3A R882H**. The NPM1 VAF of 39% is consistent with heterozygous mutation in most of the cells.
5. **Clinical context.** There was no prior cytotoxic therapy and no known antecedent hematologic disorder, which fits de novo disease. None of the reports describe myelodysplasia-related cytogenetic changes or myelodysplasia-related gene mutations; for example, ASXL1, SRSF2, U2AF1, SF3B1, EZH2, BCOR, STAG2 and ZRSR2 were all negative.

## 6. Prognostic and Management Notes

These notes are limited to what the reports state:

- **NPM1 mutation:** this defines the WHO/ICC entity. The reports do not assign a formal risk category on its own.
- **FLT3-ITD:** the molecular report calls it **prognostically adverse** and a **target for FLT3 inhibitor therapy**. The allelic ratio of 0.62 is reported to support treatment decisions.
- **DNMT3A R882H:** this commonly co-occurs with NPM1 and FLT3-ITD and is often a clonal hematopoiesis founder event. It does not change the disease category and is reported for possible prognostic relevance.
- **Adverse-risk markers absent:** no TP53 mutation or 17p deletion, no −5/5q or −7/7q, no KMT2A or MECOM rearrangement, and no complex karyotype.
- **MRD monitoring:** the flow report identifies the aberrant CD56/CD7 phenotype as useful for flow-based MRD tracking.
- **Not in the source reports:** none of them assigns a formal integrated risk category (such as ELN), so this report does not state one. Formal risk assignment and treatment planning rest with the treating hematologist.
